Frequently Asked Questions

🔒 Is my trading data safe and private?

Yes. Your trading data is encrypted and stored in Google Cloud Singapore (asia-southeast1) with strict multi-tenant security isolation. Nobody—not even platform administrators—can access your private strategy setups or P&L.

💳 How does payment and cancellation work?

We support both automated monthly subscription via Stripe (Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Debit Cards) and Touch 'n Go / DuitNow QR. You can cancel your subscription at any time with one click in your account settings—no questions asked and no lock-in contracts.

⚡ Why manual logging instead of broker API connection?

Broker API connections often pose severe account credential security risks. Furthermore, professional hedge fund research shows that the physical act of logging a trade with Pre-Flight checks builds 80% higher psychological discipline than passive automatic syncing.

📊 What happens when I reach the 20-trade free limit?
